How to Apply for a Gambian National ID Card

This guide explains the main stages of applying for a Gambian National Identity Card. Applicants should prepare their identity documents and check the current requirements before visiting the Immigration Department.

Before You Go

The Gambian National Identity Card is intended for eligible Gambian citizens aged 18 and above. Applicants must provide evidence of identity and undergo screening to establish their Gambian nationality.

What to Bring

Prepare the documents that apply to your situation before travelling to the enrolment centre.

Birth Certificate and Identity Evidence

Bring your Gambian birth certificate, valid Gambian passport, or another accepted identity document. If you do not have these documents, ask officials about the alternative verification process.

Parents' Supporting Documents

Be prepared to answer questions about your parents and your nationality. If officials request supporting documents from your parents, confirm which documents they need before attending.

At the Immigration Department

Attend the designated enrolment centre with your application form and supporting documents. Officials will examine your documents and may interview you to verify your claim to Gambian nationality. Follow their instructions during the application process.

After Your Application

Follow the instructions provided by the officials about any further checks and when or where to collect your card. If a document is missing, ask what additional evidence is acceptable rather than guessing.
